# Mukherjee et al., The Alpha 21364 Network Architecture

Written by the designers, this article describes the on-chip router, the 2D torus, the packet classes and virtual channels, and the deadlock-avoidance argument. Its first page gives the chip’s headline numbers.

[Read the paper](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~markhill/restricted/ieeemicro02_21364network.pdf) · [Publication page](https://doi.org/10.1109/40.988687).

Published in IEEE Micro 22(1), 2002, pp. 26–35. Page numbers are the printed ones.

## Where to look

- **Chip overview.** Page 26: transistor count, process, clock, bandwidths, and the 128-processor maximum. Figure 1 on p. 27 shows the floor plan.
- **Router ports and packets.** Figure 2 on p. 27 and pp. 27–28.
- **Topology and routing.** Page 28 and Figure 3 on p. 29.
- **Deadlock avoidance.** Pages 28–31, Figures 4 and 5.
- **Router pipeline.** Page 31 and Figure 6; Table 1 on p. 32.
