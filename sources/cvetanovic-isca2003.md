# Cvetanovic, Performance Analysis of the Alpha 21364-based HP GS1280 Multiprocessor

This is the performance source. It measures a GS1280 against the previous-generation GS320 and ES45 on latency, bandwidth, SPEC CPU2000 and applications, and its overview is the clearest statement of the L2, memory controller and link parameters. The author worked for HP.

[Read the paper](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~rajeev/cs7820/papers/alpha21364.pdf) · [Publication page](https://doi.org/10.1145/859618.859643).

Published at ISCA 2003, pp. 218–228. The open PDF has no printed page numbers; PDF page *n* is proceedings page 217 + *n*.

## Where to look

- **System overview.** Section 2, PDF p. 2: L2, memory controllers, links, coherence protocol, and the GS320 comparison.
- **Latency and bandwidth.** Sections 3.1–3.2, PDF pp. 3–4, Figures 4–7.
- **SPEC CPU2000.** Section 3.3, PDF pp. 4–5, Figures 8–11.
- **Remote latency.** Figure 13, PDF p. 5.
- **Conclusions.** Sections 7–8 and Figure 28, PDF p. 10.
