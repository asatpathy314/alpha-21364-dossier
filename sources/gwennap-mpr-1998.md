# Gwennap, Alpha 21364 to Ease Memory Bottleneck

Microprocessor Report’s coverage of the 1998 announcement explains why the chip was designed the way it was: the memory-latency problem, the reuse of the 21264 core, the on-chip L2, the Rambus controllers, the directory in local memory, and the mesh. The numbers are 1998 targets.

[Read the article](https://www.ardent-tool.com/CPU/docs/MPR/19981026/121402.pdf).

Microprocessor Report, 26 October 1998, four pages.

## Where to look

- **Motivation.** Page 1.
- **Core reuse and latency targets.** Page 2, with Figure 1.
- **L2, memory controllers and directory.** Pages 2–3, with the sidebar on RDRAM channels and Figure 2.
- **Network and I/O.** Pages 3–4.
