# STREAM benchmark results for the GS1280

The STREAM site at the University of Virginia lists sustained memory bandwidth for systems submitted by their vendors. HP submitted GS1280 results in 2003 and 2004, and the 64-processor result sits in the all-time top 20 table. The measurements were run by HP, but the benchmark and the table are maintained independently.

[Top 20 table](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/top20/Bandwidth.html) · [Standard results table](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/standard/Bandwidth.html) · [STREAM home](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/).

**Supplementary performance source. Published benchmark results, vendor-run. Open.**

## Where to look

- **Top 20.** The row “HP_AlphaServer_GS1280-1300,” 64 CPUs, submitted 11 August 2004: Triad 431,450 MB/s.
- **Scaling.** The standard results table has GS1280 rows at 1300 MHz for 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 CPUs (June and August 2004) and at 1150 MHz for 1 to 64 CPUs (January and August 2003). Single-processor Triad is about 7.2 GB/s at 1300 MHz and 6.3 GB/s at 1150 MHz.
- **Submission notes.** The mailing-list posts record the compiler flags and OpenMP setup: [January 2003](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/stream_mail/2003/0006.html), [August 2003](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/stream_mail/2003/0016.html), [June 2004](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/stream_mail/2004/0015.html), [August 2004](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/stream/stream_mail/2004/0027.html).
