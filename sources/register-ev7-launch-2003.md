# The Register, EV7 AlphaServers unleashed as chip line heads into sunset

This launch story gives the context for the 21364’s arrival: HP’s announcement of the ES47, ES80 and GS1280 systems in January 2003, the clock speeds and prices, the IBM fab, and the plan to end the Alpha line after one more revision.

[Read the article](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2003/01/21/ev7-alphaservers-unleashed-as-chip-line-heads-into-sunset/1414298).

**Secondary source. Trade press. Open.** By Timothy Prickett Morgan via ComputerWire, 21 January 2003.

## Where to look

- **Systems and prices.** The ES47 (two processors), ES80, and GS1280 (up to 64 processors) with their starting prices.
- **Chip facts.** EV7 at 1 GHz and 1.15 GHz with a 1.75 MB L2, fabricated by IBM Microelectronics.
- **The sunset.** The EV79 shrink promised for late 2004 and HP’s plan to move customers to Itanium.
